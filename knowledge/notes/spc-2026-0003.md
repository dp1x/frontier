# FROST (RFC 9591) — normative extraction note

## 1. Why this spec analysis

`msn-2026-0013` (active) targets a byte-exact cross-implementation audit of three
FROST implementations — ZKSecurity frost (Go reference), ZCashFoundation
frost-rs (Rust), ZCashFoundation frost-dkg (Rust companion) — against the
public RFC 9591 Appendix A KAT vectors for the **secp256k1** and **ed25519**
parameter sets, plus 8 negative stimuli (nonce reuse, off-curve R, s outside
[1, n-1], threshold dropout, etc.). The byte-exact differential needs a
deterministic normative oracle; this document supplies it by extracting every
normative step from RFC 9591 with section citations and flagging the most
likely divergence sites as explicit ambiguities rather than resolving them
silently.

Sources: RFC 9591 (HTML + canonical TXT, both fetched 2026-08-31) at
https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9591. Normative refs to RFC 8032, RFC 9380,
RFC 9496 where the ciphersuites inherit their primitives.

## 2. Ciphersuites in scope

- **§6.1 FROST(Ed25519, SHA-512)** — contextString = `FROST-ED25519-SHA512-v1`.
  H1/H3/H4/H5 use `contextString || "rho"/"nonce"/"msg"/"com"`; **H2 has no
  contextString** for RFC 8032 compatibility. Ed25519 verification uses the
  cofactored equation `[8][z]B = [8]R + [8][c]PK`. **HIGH-DIVERGENCE RISK:
  the missing contextString on H2 (AMB-E.5-007) and the [8]-cofactored check
  (AMB-E.5-006).**

- **§6.5 FROST(secp256k1, SHA-256)** — contextString =
  `FROST-secp256k1-SHA256-v1`. H1/H2/H3 use RFC 9380 `hash_to_field(m, 1)`
  with `expand_message_xmd`, SHA-256, DST = `contextString || "rho"/"chal"/"nonce"`,
  L=48. H4/H5 are SHA-256 with `contextString || "msg"/"com"` prefix.
  Verification per Appendix B (prime_order_verify).

The other three ciphersuites (ristretto255 §6.2, Ed448 §6.3, P-256 §6.4) are
documented in the YAML for completeness but are **out of scope** for
msn-2026-0013.

## 3. Per-step normative extraction (with citations)

### 3.1 §4.1 nonce_generate
`nonce = H3(random_bytes(32) || SerializeScalar(secret))`. The 32-byte CSPRNG
sample is the freshness hedge; H3 domain-separator is `contextString || "nonce"`.
Fresh randomness MUST be CSPRNG-sourced (§7.3) for nonce-reuse resistance.

### 3.2 §5.1 commit (round 1)
`commit(sk_i)` returns `(nonces, comms) = ((hiding_nonce, binding_nonce),
(hiding_nonce_commitment, binding_nonce_commitment))` where
`hiding_nonce = nonce_generate(sk_i)`,
`binding_nonce = nonce_generate(sk_i)` (distinct fresh randomness),
`hiding_nonce_commitment = ScalarBaseMult(hiding_nonce)`,
`binding_nonce_commitment = ScalarBaseMult(binding_nonce)`. The order is
canonical: **hiding first, binding second**.

### 3.3 §4.5 compute_group_commitment
For each `(id, hiding_nonce_commitment, binding_nonce_commitment)` in
ascending-by-id order:
`binding_nonce = ScalarMult(binding_nonce_commitment, binding_factor)`;
`group_commitment += hiding_nonce_commitment + binding_nonce`. Starts at
`Identity()`. Naive O(n); the multi-exp optimization from §4.5 last paragraph
is NOT RECOMMENDED.

### 3.4 §4.4 compute_binding_factors (binding factor `rho_i`)
`rho_input_prefix = SerializeElement(group_public_key) || H4(msg) ||
H5(encode_group_commitment_list(commitment_list))`. For each participant:
`rho_input = rho_input_prefix || SerializeScalar(id)`; `binding_factor = H1(rho_input)`.
**No length-prefix** on the identifier — it's ciphersuite-fixed Ns bytes.
**HIGH-DIVERGENCE RISK: AMB-E.5-001 (length-prefix confusion) and
AMB-E.5-002 (sort comparator).**

### 3.5 §4.6 compute_challenge
`challenge_input = SerializeElement(group_commitment) ||
SerializeElement(group_public_key) || msg` (raw msg, NOT H4(msg));
`challenge = H2(challenge_input)`.

### 3.6 §5.2 sign (signature share)
`sig_share = hiding_nonce + (binding_nonce * binding_factor) + (lambda_i * sk_i * challenge)`.
Equivalent under msn-2026-0013's variable names (d=hiding, e=binding,
rho=binding_factor): `z_i = d_i + rho_i * e_i + lambda_i * sk_i * challenge`.
**Fixed order: d_i first, then rho*e, then lambda*sk*challenge.** `lambda_i`
is the Lagrange basis `derive_interpolating_value(participant_list, identifier)`
computed over the x-coordinates of the actually-responding participants.

### 3.7 §5.3 aggregate
Recomputes `binding_factor_list` and `group_commitment` identically to §5.2.
`z = sum(z_i)`; returns `(R=group_commitment, z)`. R is NOT separately summed —
it's the group_commitment computation result.

### 3.8 Appendix B verification (prime-order ciphersuites)
`prime_order_verify(msg, sig=(R,z), PK)`:
`c = H2(SerializeElement(R) || SerializeElement(PK) || msg)`;
`l = ScalarBaseMult(z)`; `r = R + ScalarMult(PK, c)`; return `l == r`. The
verification equation is `[z]B = R + [c]PK`. Ed25519/Ed448 MUST check the
cofactored form `[cofactor][z]B = [cofactor]R + [cofactor][c]PK` (§6.1, §6.3).

## 4. RFC 9591 Appendix E KAT vectors

Five vectors, one per ciphersuite, all using the same participant structure:

| Ciphersuite | Section | n | t | signing | IDs | message |
|-------------|---------|---|---|---------|-----|---------|
| Ed25519, SHA-512 | E.1 | 3 | 2 | 2 | {1, 3} | `74657374` (ASCII "test") |
| Ed448, SHAKE256 | E.2 | 3 | 2 | 2 | {1, 3} | `74657374` |
| ristretto255, SHA-512 | E.3 | 3 | 2 | 2 | {1, 3} | `74657374` |
| P-256, SHA-256 | E.4 | 3 | 2 | 2 | {1, 3} | `74657374` |
| secp256k1, SHA-256 | E.5 | 3 | 2 | 2 | {1, 3} | `74657374` |

The KAT vectors are **public and reproducible from RFC text alone** — they are
embedded verbatim in Appendix E.1-E.5 of the published RFC, not in external
files. **AMB-E.5-008**: the msn-2026-0013 acceptance criterion of
`>= 3 secp256k1 + >= 3 ed25519 vectors` cannot be met from RFC 9591 alone
(one vector per ciphersuite); the remaining vectors MUST come from a fixed-seed
implementation harness.

The secp256k1 (E.5) and Ed25519 (E.1) vectors are reproduced verbatim in
`spc-2026-0003.yaml` under `specifications[].normative_clauses` for the
respective sections.

## 5. Negative-stimulus normative behavior

Per `spc-2026-0003.yaml::negative_stimulus_matrix.stimuli`:

| Stimulus | RFC 9591 normative behavior | Citation |
|----------|------------------------------|----------|
| Nonce reuse (same `(d_i, e_i)`) | §5.1 forbids; §7.3 Coordinator MAY track; per-impl rejection is implementation-defined (AMB-E.5-003) | §5.1, §7.3 |
| Off-curve R | DeserializeElement MUST reject (§3.1, §6.4, §6.5) | §3.1, §6.5 |
| z outside `[1, n-1]` | DeserializeScalar rejects `[n, 2^Ns-1]`; prime_order_verify catches the rest; no explicit MUST on `[1, n-1]` (AMB-E.5-004) | §3.1, Appendix B |
| Threshold dropout | §5.4: no robustness; Coordinator SHOULD abort, MAY retry (AMB-E.5-005) | §5.4 |
| Duplicate participant commitment | §4.2 derive_interpolating_value raises `invalid parameters` on duplicate x_j | §4.2 |
| Commitment with identity element | SerializeElement raises on identity (§3.1); DeserializeElement raises on identity (§3.1) | §3.1 |
| Wrong contextString in binding_factor | §6.6: per-suite contextString MUST — produces byte-different (correct) output | §6.6 |
| Un-cofactored Ed25519 verification | §6.1 MUST use `[8][z]B = [8]R + [8][c]PK` (AMB-E.5-006) | §6.1 |

## 6. Ambiguities (preserved, not silently resolved)

Ten ambiguities are documented in `spc-2026-0003.yaml::ambiguities`. The
highest-likelihood cross-impl divergence sites are:

1. **AMB-E.5-001**: `SerializeScalar(id)` vs length-prefixed encoding for the
   binding_factor_input tail.
2. **AMB-E.5-002**: commitment_list ascending-by-id sort comparator (implicit
   "least nonnegative mod p" representation).
3. **AMB-E.5-003**: per-impl nonce-reuse detection (no MUST).
4. **AMB-E.5-004**: aggregate z range vs per-share z range.
5. **AMB-E.5-005**: threshold dropout is out of spec; implementations may retry.
6. **AMB-E.5-006**: Ed25519 MUST use `[8]`-cofactored check; need per-impl verify.
7. **AMB-E.5-007**: Ed25519 H2 has NO contextString (RFC 8032 compat); libraries
   that "be safe" and add it diverge.
8. **AMB-E.5-008**: single KAT per ciphersuite; need harness for >= 3 vectors.
9. **AMB-E.5-009**: ristretto255 Scalar-from-uniform routine (out of scope but
   documented).
10. **AMB-E.5-010**: `lambda_i` caching across distinct participant subsets.
